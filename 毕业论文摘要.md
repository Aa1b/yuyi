# 毕业论文摘要

## 中文摘要

为满足用户在日常场景中对生活记录、内容管理及轻量社交的核心需求，本研究设计并实现了一套基于微信小程序的生活记录管理系统。考虑到移动端轻量化与跨平台特性，系统采用前后端分离架构与RESTful接口规范进行开发，重点实现生活记录发布、社交互动及消息通知等核心功能模块。

研究实施过程分为四个阶段：首先，通过需求分析明确系统定位，完成总体设计，确定微信小程序客户端与Node.js服务端的职责边界，并设计MySQL数据库表结构以支撑数据存储；其次，客户端层面实现登录鉴权、记录列表与详情展示、内容发布与编辑、搜索、关注关系管理及消息通知等功能模块，同时对各类网络请求进行统一封装与错误处理，提升交互稳定性；再次，服务端基于JWT技术实现身份认证，通过MySQL完成数据持久化，并利用数据库触发器维护点赞数、评论数与通知数据的一致性，此外针对多媒体资源（图片、视频）进行压缩、缩略图生成及封面提取等优化处理；最后，通过接口联调与真机测试验证系统功能完整性与运行稳定性，并基于测试结果完成系统优化与部署。

本系统在微信开发者工具与Node.js环境中开发，集成Express框架、MySQL数据库等开源技术，最终实现的生活记录管理系统具备良好的可用性与可扩展性。

**关键词**：微信小程序；生活记录管理系统；前后端分离；RESTful API；JWT认证；MySQL数据库

## 英文摘要（Abstract）

To meet users’ core needs for life recording, content management, and lightweight social interaction in daily scenarios, this study designs and implements a life record management system based on WeChat Mini Program. Considering the lightweight and cross-platform nature of mobile terminals, the system adopts a front-end and back-end separation architecture and RESTful API specification, and focuses on core modules including life record publishing, social interaction, and message notification.

The implementation is carried out in four stages: requirement analysis and overall design with client-server responsibility boundaries and MySQL schema design; client-side modules for authentication, record list and detail, publishing and editing, search, follow management, and notifications, with unified request encapsulation and error handling; server-side JWT authentication, MySQL persistence, database triggers for like/comment/notification consistency, and multimedia processing (compression, thumbnails, video covers). Finally, interface integration and real-device testing verify completeness and stability, and optimization and deployment are completed based on test results.

The system is developed with WeChat Developer Tools and Node.js, using Express and MySQL. The resulting life record management system achieves good usability and extensibility.

**Keywords**: WeChat Mini Program; life record management system; front-end and back-end separation; RESTful API; JWT authentication; MySQL database
